A303790 cubic scalar period certificate
========================================

Hamiltonian:
    K(p,q) = p^2 + q^2 + p^3 + q^3

Energy curve:
    K(p,q) = E

Period form:
    omega_E = 2*dq / K_p
            = 2*dq / (p*(3*p+2))

Energy derivative at fixed q:
    D_E = (1/K_p)*partial_p

Picard-Fuchs operator:
    L_E = A2(E)*D_E^2 + A1(E)*D_E + A0(E)

where
    A2(E) = E*(27*E - 8)*(27*E - 4)
    A1(E) = 2187*E**2 - 648*E + 32
    A0(E) = 15*(27*E - 4)

Certificate function:
    Xi(E,p,q) = V(E,p,q) / K_p^3

with

V(E,p,q) =
-4374*E**2*p*q - 1458*E**2*p - 1458*E**2*q - 486*E*p**2*q - 162*E*p**2 + 7290*E*p*q**4 + 9720*E*p*q**3 + 2430*E*p*q**2 + 432*E*p*q + 306*E*p + 2430*E*q**4 + 2268*E*q**3 - 162*E*q**2 + 144*E*q + 30*E - 648*p**2*q**4 - 864*p**2*q**3 + 264*p**2*q + 34*p**2 - 648*p*q**5 - 2592*p*q**4 - 2538*p*q**3 - 402*p*q**2 + 192*p*q + 486*q**6 + 756*q**5 - 378*q**4 - 774*q**3 - 62*q**2 + 64*q

Exact identity on K=E:
    L_E(2/K_p)
      = partial_q Xi - (K_q/K_p)*partial_p Xi.

Equivalently:
    L_E(omega_E) = d Xi

on the energy curve.

Therefore, for every closed cycle gamma_E avoiding the poles,
    L_E integral_(gamma_E) omega_E = 0.

Verification:
    numerator remainder modulo K-E = 0
