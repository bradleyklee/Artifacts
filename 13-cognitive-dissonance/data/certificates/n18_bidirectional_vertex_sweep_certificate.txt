PHYSICAL GEOMETRIC CONVENTION (CENTER-SITE COORDINATES)
  A stored cell coordinate (i,j) is the CENTER of the unit square
      Q(i,j) = [i-1/2, i+1/2] x [j-1/2, j+1/2].
  Its four physical vertices are
      (i-1/2,j-1/2), (i-1/2,j+1/2),
      (i+1/2,j-1/2), (i+1/2,j+1/2).

INTERNAL DOUBLED-VERTEX COORDINATES
  The sweep internally stores every physical point p as p2 = 2p.
  Therefore:
      physical circle center C = C2 / 2
      physical squared radius r^2 = r2^2 / 4
  where C2 and r2^2 are the raw doubled-coordinate values.

STANDARD LOWER-LEFT-CORNER GRID CONVENTION
  To draw the same geometry with integer square corners, translate every
  physical point by (+1/2,+1/2). The circle center becomes
      C_standard = C + (1/2,1/2),
  while r^2 is unchanged.
