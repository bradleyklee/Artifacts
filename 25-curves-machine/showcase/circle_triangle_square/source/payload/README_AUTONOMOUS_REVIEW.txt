AUTONOMOUS REFEREE PAYLOAD
==========================

This payload accompanies "Circle Triangle Square Periods certificate".

Run:

    python3 verify_certificate.py

Dependencies:

    Python 3.11+
    sympy
    mpmath

The verifier checks:

1. the displayed curve, rho, Picard-Fuchs operator, factorization, and exact
   differential certificate A_alpha(omega)=d(V/rho^3);
2. the two centers printed inline on the page:

       gamma_z^(1) subset (p,q) around (0,0),
       gamma_z^(2) subset (ip,q) around (0,-1);

3. the exact p-Wick expansion at the tunneling center c=(0,-1);
4. the p-Wick Hamiltonian Hessian, harmonic frequency sqrt(5)/2, and full
   dephased harmonic period 4*pi/sqrt(5);
5. the integer series printed on the page, including

       T2(1/4-100x)
       = 1+108x+24420x^2+6819120x^3+2096600100x^4+...;

6. the Picard-Fuchs equation for both printed series;
7. the page-normalized Abel identity with constant sqrt(5)/pi;
8. numerical quadrature of the full green p-Wick oval against the local T2
   series near z=1/4.

NORMALIZATION CORRECTION
------------------------

The older saddle-normalized solution satisfied

    T2_saddle(1/8)=1,
    T2_saddle(1/4)=sqrt(7/10).

That value implies only 2*pi/sqrt(5) after applying the old prefactor, one
half of the full green oval's harmonic period.  The page now uses the full
p-Wick oval centered at (0,-1):

    T2 = sqrt(10/7) T2_saddle,
    T2(1/4)=1,
    T2(z)=sqrt(5)/(4*pi) integral_gamma i*omega.

For alpha=2H, the p-Wick Hamiltonian H(iU,-1+Q) has quadratic part

    1/8 - (5/4)U^2 - (1/4)Q^2,

so its harmonic frequency is sqrt(5)/2 and its full period is
4*pi/sqrt(5).  This is also verified numerically from the full green oval.
