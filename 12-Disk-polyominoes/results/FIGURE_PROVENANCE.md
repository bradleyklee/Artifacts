# Figure provenance

`circle_vs_axis_ellipse_tri_tet_pent.svg` and its PNG preview were drawn from
lattice-site coordinates and rational certificates, not generated from an image
model.  The companion certificate table records the conic equations and circle
centers/radii used by the figure.

For the figure only, floating point is used after verification to sample the
already-certified circle and ellipse curves for SVG/PNG drawing.  It is not used
to decide inclusion or exclusion.
