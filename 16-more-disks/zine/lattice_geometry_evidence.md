## Controls and threshold

| family | exhaustive finite result |
|---|---|
| squares, `L=2,N=4` | 256 cardinal/lattice starts; no cap survivor |
| dodecagons, ordinary two-body lattice starts | 96 starts at `L=2` and 576 starts at `L=3`; no cap survivor through 100 batches |

The selected dodecagon display seed is the lexicographically minimal ternary
representative: central face 1, incoming `(E,N)`. It has full independently
replayed certificates through 2,000 batches and an independently replayed
compact checkpoint through 7,500 batches.

The two selected 24-gon records begin at sites `[0,1]` with velocity pairs
`(E,S)` and `(W,N)`. Direct velocity negation at those sites swaps the two
classes. Each has a full independently replayed 100-batch certificate.
