This is the earlier L=2 search packet staged during the transition from
Artifact 15. The catalogue has a central `B` site plus four surrounding `A`
sites; it is **not** the four-quadrant geometry in the sibling directory.

6 rejects, 7 crossings of the 129-bit state-complexity threshold, and 12
low-complexity observation prefixes. These are preliminary findings and need
independent reproduction/audit.
