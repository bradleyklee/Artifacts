%C Derived from the "Ternatree counting" matrix-reduction algorithm of Harm.On.ica S-O-L.
%C The generating function A(x) satisfies the algebraic equation A(x)^3 = 4*A(x) - 3 - x, with A(0)=1.
%C Setting T(x) = A(x) - 1 gives T = x + 3*T^2 + T^3, showing that a(n) counts rooted planar trees in which every internal node has out-degree 2 (in 3 possible "colors") or out-degree 3.
%C The recurrence is obtained by canceling columns 1&2, 1&3, 2&3 of the 2x3 matrix X built from the plum-root reduction matrices U and V, then normalizing the cross-products.
%C P0(n) = -3*(3*n-1)*(3*n+1), P1(n) = -81*(n+1)*(2*n+1), P2(n) = 13*(n+1)*(n+2).
%C The differential operator factors the singularity at x = (-81+48*sqrt(3))/13.
