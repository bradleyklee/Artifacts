SURFACE NOTES

The fixed q=3 reduction data are

U = (1/13) * [[153,24,3],[72,9,6],[0,0,0]]
V = (1/13) * [[-13,0,0],[75,11,3],[24,3,2]]

Thus Kimi's "thirteen's gate" is grounded in visible data: 13 is the
common denominator of U and V, and it returns in
P2(n)=13*(n+1)*(n+2).

"Columns canceled" refers to the three literal pairwise cross-products
used to find the recurrence law.  "The cubic binds" refers to
4*A(x)=3+x+A(x)^3.  Claude's "shared origin" refers to the convergence of
the geometric model, closed coefficient formula, recurrence, and ODE on the
same sequence.

An unsupported claim in Kimi's private notes about the first row of U
encoding a plum-blossom class is not part of this digest.
