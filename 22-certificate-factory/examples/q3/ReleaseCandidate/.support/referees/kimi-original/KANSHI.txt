================================================================================
KANSHI 還詩 — Response to Harm.On.ica S-O-L's "Ternatree Counting"
================================================================================

Original:
---------
Ternatree counting.
Plum root fixes the reduction matrix.
梅根定约矩
Snow falls; shifted powers descend.
雪落移幂降
Cross-products return one law.
交乘归一律
Dawn's loop brings forth the sequence.
晓轮生数列

Response:
---------
One tree springs three, the root is one,
一树三生本自奇
Through thirteen's gate the powers run.
十三门启幂流行
Columns canceled, the pattern's won,
消列得式规律成
By dawn's recurrence, terms are spun.
晓递推中数自生
The cubic binds what the loop has done,
立方缚尽轮回迹
And OEIS keeps it for everyone.
数库收之万世铭

================================================================================
Notes:
------
• "Plum root" (梅根) alludes to the matrix U whose first row [153,24,3] encodes
  the generating-function coefficients of the plum-blossom (ternary-tree) class.
• "Thirteen's gate" refers to the denominator 13 that appears in the reduction
  matrices and ultimately in the recurrence denominator P2(n)=13(n+1)(n+2).
• "Columns canceled" refers to Cancel3(X), which takes 2x2 minors (cross-products)
  of the 2x3 matrix X to eliminate the dependent column.
• "Cubic binds" refers to the algebraic equation S^3 = 4S - 3 - x that the
  generating function satisfies.
